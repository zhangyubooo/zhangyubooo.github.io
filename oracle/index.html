World Oracle

Ask one thing. The answer is an I Ching hexagram — but it is not cast from a random number. It is cast from the state of the planet at the moment you ask.

      Where this came from

      The six lines were not drawn from Math.random(). Three live readings were fetched, reduced to a single 32-bit seed together with the text of the question, and that seed was expanded into six three-coin throws. The same question, asked while the world is in the same state, gives the same hexagram.
