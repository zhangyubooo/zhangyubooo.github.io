About

Hi again!

This is Yubo. I've always been curious about new technologies and the different ways they can change how we design. I like moving between interfaces, physical objects, and spaces, because each one gives me a different way to think about interaction. When I come across a new tool or technology, my first reaction is usually to wonder what I could make with it, how it could change an experience, and where else it could be applied. I enjoy learning by experimenting, and I hope to keep exploring new technologies not just as tools, but as new materials for design.
